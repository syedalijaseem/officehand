# Juniper Ridge Builders – Standard Subcontract Terms

**Applies to:** all subcontractors working on Juniper Ridge Builders projects
**Version:** 2026.1

*Fictional sample document for the OfficeHand demo.*

## 1. Scope

1.1 Subcontractor will perform the work described in each project's scope sheet, following the project plans and specifications.

## 2. Contract Documents

2.1 These terms, the project scope sheet, the plans and specifications, and any written change orders form the subcontract.

## 3. Schedule

3.1 Subcontractor will keep to the project schedule set by Juniper Ridge Builders and give at least 48 hours' notice of any expected delay.

## 4. Payment

4.1 Juniper Ridge Builders pays approved invoices **Net 30** from the date an approved invoice is received.

4.2 Juniper Ridge Builders holds **10% retainage** on each payment. Retainage is released after final completion and acceptance of the subcontractor's work.

4.3 Each invoice must include a **conditional lien waiver** for the amount invoiced. An unconditional lien waiver is due once payment clears.

## 5. Invoicing

5.1 Invoices must show the project ID or address, a description of work completed, and the invoice number.

## 6. Changes

6.1 Juniper Ridge Builders will **not pay for extra work unless a written change order was approved in advance** by Juniper Ridge Builders.

6.2 Requests for change orders must include a price and any schedule impact.

## 7. Safety

7.1 Subcontractor is responsible for the safety of its crew and must follow all applicable safety regulations.

## 8. Insurance

8.1 Subcontractor will carry general liability insurance of at least **$1,000,000 per occurrence and $2,000,000 aggregate**, commercial auto insurance of at least $1,000,000, and workers' compensation as required by law.

8.2 Juniper Ridge Builders must be named as an additional insured on the general liability policy.

## 9. Warranty

9.1 Subcontractor warrants its work for one (1) year from the project's substantial completion.

## 10. Termination

10.1 Juniper Ridge Builders may terminate the subcontract for failure to perform after seven (7) days' written notice.

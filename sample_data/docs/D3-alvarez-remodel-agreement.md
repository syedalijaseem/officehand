# Alvarez Kitchen & Addition – Remodel Agreement

**Project:** P-102 Alvarez Kitchen & Addition, 88 Fernhill Ave, Alder Falls, WA
**Contractor:** Juniper Ridge Builders, LLC ("Contractor")
**Owner:** Rosa Alvarez ("Owner")
**Agreement date:** August 18, 2026

*Fictional sample document for the OfficeHand demo.*

## Section 1 – Scope

1.1 Contractor will remodel the existing kitchen and build a 450 square foot addition for a family room, as shown in the drawings dated August 4, 2026.

## Section 2 – Contract Price

2.1 Owner will pay Contractor a Contract Price of **$268,500.00**, which includes the allowances in Section 6.

## Section 3 – Payments

3.1 Payments are due within ten (10) days of each invoice: 15% at signing, 25% at rough-in complete, 25% at drywall complete, 25% at cabinets installed, and 10% at substantial completion.

## Section 4 – Permits and Inspections

4.1 Contractor will obtain the building permit and schedule all inspections.

## Section 5 – Changes

5.1 Changes must be agreed in a written change order signed by Owner and Contractor before the changed work begins.

5.2 Each change order states the price and any change to the completion date.

5.3 Changed work is priced at Contractor's cost plus a markup of **12%**.

## Section 6 – Allowances

6.1 The Contract Price includes these allowances:

| Item | Allowance |
|---|---|
| Cabinets | $32,000.00 |
| Countertops | $9,500.00 |
| Appliances | $11,000.00 |
| Backsplash and floor tile | $4,800.00 |

6.2 Costs above an allowance are billed to Owner at cost plus 12%. Costs below an allowance are credited at cost.

## Section 7 – Schedule

7.1 Work begins on September 8, 2026. Contractor will reach substantial completion by **January 15, 2027**, extended for Owner changes, hidden conditions found during demolition, and supply delays.

## Section 8 – Warranty

8.1 Contractor warrants its workmanship for one (1) year from substantial completion.

# Hendricks Residence – Construction Agreement

**Project:** P-101 Hendricks Residence, 412 Cedar Loop, Alder Falls, WA
**Builder:** Juniper Ridge Builders, LLC ("Builder")
**Owner:** Laura Hendricks and Tom Hendricks ("Owner")
**Agreement date:** March 2, 2026

*Fictional sample document for the OfficeHand demo.*

## Article 1 – Scope of Work

1.1 Builder will construct a single-family residence of approximately 3,400 square feet, with an attached two-car garage, as shown in the Plans dated February 12, 2026 and described in the Specifications & Finish Schedule.

1.2 Work includes all labor, materials, equipment, permits and supervision needed to complete the residence, except items listed as Owner-supplied in Article 5.

## Article 2 – Contract Documents

2.1 The Contract Documents are this Agreement, the Plans, the Specifications & Finish Schedule, and any Change Orders signed after the date of this Agreement.

2.2 If the documents conflict, this Agreement controls, then signed Change Orders, then the Specifications, then the Plans.

## Article 3 – Contract Sum

3.1 Owner will pay Builder a fixed Contract Sum of **$1,185,000.00**, which includes the allowances in Article 7.

3.2 The Contract Sum changes only by a signed Change Order (Article 6) or an allowance adjustment (Article 7).

## Article 4 – Payment Schedule

4.1 Builder will invoice each draw when the milestone is reached. Owner will pay each invoice within ten (10) days of receipt.

| Draw | Milestone | Percent | Amount |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Deposit at signing | 10% | $118,500.00 |
| 2 | Foundation complete | 15% | $177,750.00 |
| 3 | Framing complete | 15% | $177,750.00 |
| 4 | Dry-in (roof, windows, exterior doors) | 20% | $237,000.00 |
| 5 | Drywall complete | 20% | $237,000.00 |
| 6 | Finishes complete | 15% | $177,750.00 |
| 7 | Retainage, due at substantial completion | 5% | $59,250.00 |

4.2 Late payments accrue interest at 1% per month.

## Article 5 – Owner Responsibilities

5.1 Owner will provide access to the property, make selections by the dates in the selection schedule, and supply the following items: window coverings and the refrigerator water filter system.

5.2 Late selections that delay the work extend the schedule day for day.

## Article 6 – Changes in the Work

6.1 Any change to the work must be documented in a written Change Order describing the change, the price and any schedule impact.

6.2 A Change Order is effective only when signed by both Owner and Builder.

6.3 Builder will not start changed work until the Change Order is signed, except for emergency work needed to protect people or property.

6.4 The price of changed work is Builder's cost plus a markup of **15% for overhead and profit**.

6.5 Credits for deleted work are given at Builder's cost, without markup.

## Article 7 – Allowances

7.1 An allowance is an amount included in the Contract Sum for an item whose final selection is made later by Owner.

7.2 Allowance Schedule:

| Item | Allowance |
|---|---|
| Appliances | $18,000.00 |
| Lighting fixtures | $6,500.00 |
| Tile (material only) | $14,000.00 |
| Countertops (fabricated and installed) | $16,500.00 |
| Plumbing fixtures | $9,800.00 |
| Landscaping | $12,000.00 |

7.3 If the actual cost of an allowance item exceeds the allowance, Owner will pay the difference plus 15%. If the actual cost is lower, Builder will credit the difference at cost.

## Article 8 – Insurance

8.1 Builder will carry general liability insurance of at least $1,000,000 per occurrence and builder's risk insurance for the full value of the work.

## Article 9 – Schedule

9.1 Work will begin on or about April 6, 2026. Builder will achieve substantial completion by **June 30, 2027**, subject to extensions under this Article.

9.2 The schedule is extended for delays caused by weather, Owner changes, late Owner selections, permitting delays, or supply shortages outside Builder's control.

## Article 10 – Inspections

10.1 Builder will schedule all required municipal inspections. Owner may walk the site with Builder by appointment.

## Article 11 – Warranty

11.1 Builder warrants its workmanship for **one (1) year** from the date of substantial completion.

11.2 Manufacturer warranties on appliances, fixtures and materials pass through to Owner.

## Article 12 – Disputes

12.1 The parties will first try to resolve any dispute by direct discussion, then by mediation in Alder County, before any other action.
